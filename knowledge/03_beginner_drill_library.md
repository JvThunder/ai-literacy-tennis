# Beginner Drill Library
Source: practice planning cards (sample document)

## Shadow turn and point
No court or ball needed. Stand side-on to a mirror or wall and turn the shoulders until the non-racket hand points at the side fence. Hold for two seconds and return. Three sets of ten repetitions train the unit turn and can be done at home in five minutes.

## Shadow swing past a marker
Place a cone or bottle about a racket length in front of the front hip. Swing slowly and brush it with the strings at contact. The marker gives a visible target for the contact point and works without a court or partner.

## Drop-feed turn and hit
A partner drops the ball from shoulder height so it bounces once. Turn before the bounce and hit crosscourt. This isolates preparation timing because the feed is slow and repeatable. Three sets of eight balls.

## Self-drop forehands
Drop the ball in front of the front hip and hit it into the service box. The player controls the feed, so there is no rush and no partner is needed. Useful for repeating the contact point alone on a court.

## Finish and freeze
Swing in slow motion and finish over the opposite shoulder, freezing for two seconds. Holding the finish gives immediate feedback on balance and on whether the swing continued through contact.

## Over-the-net-by-a-metre rally
Use a rope or imaginary line one metre above the net. Aim every forehand over the line and into the court. The target encourages a full swing and upward finish, and gives clear success counts.

## Sit and swing
Bend the knees about twenty degrees, shadow swing while staying low and rise through contact. Trains the habit of using the legs and keeps the trunk upright.

## Head-still shadow swing
Fix the eyes on a mark at head height in a mirror while swinging. The head should stay on the mark through contact. Helps beginners who lean away from the ball.
